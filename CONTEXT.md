# SemanticLab Positioning

This context defines the commercial language used to describe SemanticLab and the role of its website.

## Language

**Product Innovation Partner**:
A strategy-led, founder-led partner that helps business leaders identify valuable opportunities and carry them through product strategy, experience design, engineering, and launch.
_Avoid_: AI product studio, management consultancy, development agency

**Strategy Engagement**:
The primary commercial relationship initiated through the SemanticLab website, focused on understanding a business and identifying where intelligent products or systems can create meaningful advantage.
_Avoid_: General enquiry, product trial, sales call

**Built Product**:
A product SemanticLab has designed and delivered, presented as evidence of the studio's capability rather than as the website's primary conversion path.
_Avoid_: Product trial, portfolio piece

**Evidence-Backed Claim**:
A public statement about SemanticLab's experience, clients, outcomes, scale, pricing, or credibility that is supported by inspectable evidence and any required permission.
_Avoid_: Placeholder metric, illustrative client logo, assumed testimonial
